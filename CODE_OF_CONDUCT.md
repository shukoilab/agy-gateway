# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As researchers, systems architects, and contributors to the **Antigravity Gateway (`agy-gateway`)** project, we pledge to foster an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

We commit to creating a collaborative research and engineering environment where all participants are treated with dignity and professional respect, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy environment for scientific inquiry, systems research, and high-assurance software engineering.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive and productive environment include:

- **Empirical Rigor & Epistemic Honesty:** Ground discussions, bug reports, and code reviews in verifiable facts, benchmark data, reproducible logs, and deterministic test cases rather than conjecture.
- **Professional Courtesy & Empathy:** Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other participants, acknowledging differing levels of familiarity with distributed systems and the Agent Client Protocol (ACP).
- **Constructive Peer Review:** Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback focused strictly on code quality, architectural invariants, security perimeters, and performance budgets.
- **Collaborative Problem Solving:** Focusing on what is best for the collective research integrity, reliability, and security of the system.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting, derogatory, or ad hominem comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, doxxing, or publishing private information without explicit consent.
- Dismissive responses to technical inquiries or elitist gatekeeping.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional research and engineering setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code modifications, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions determined by other members of the project leadership.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all official project spaces (GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, code reviews, and commit messages), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---

## Reporting & Enforcement Guidelines

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported through the project's **GitHub Repository Moderation / Private Issue Reporting channels** (or via the private maintainer triage team on GitHub). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

The project team is obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Enforcement actions will follow progressive disciplinary steps:

1. **Correction:** A private written warning providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary Suspension:** A temporary ban from interacting with any project repository or discussion forum for a specified period of time.
4. **Permanent Expulsion:** A permanent ban from any interaction within the project community.
